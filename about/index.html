about me

I'm Flavio Angelo Ciani, a Computer Science student.

progris riport is my space. The name comes from Charlie Gordon's progress reports in Flowers for Algernon, the book that has stayed with me more than any other in the last few years.

My head jumps from one thing to another: university, computer science, photography, spirituality, sport, and whatever else crosses my mind. Here I write down what I actually do, think and finish, one riport at a time. A way to prove to myself (and to whoever wants to read) that I'm moving forward.
