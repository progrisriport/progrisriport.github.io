riport 1. Not 0 anymore, I want to start like Charlie, from chapter 1.

I admit I’m struggling to write. As usual my head wanders and jumps from one thought to another. Maybe what I’m realizing right now is that writing in a chat with Claude comes much more naturally to me, because instead of talking to myself I fool myself into thinking I’m talking to someone, or something (but more on that later).

This riport comes from Flowers for Algernon. I read it a while ago, and in one of my usual wandering moments I found myself going back through it, picking it apart, trying to understand what it had left me. I was thinking about riport 0, about why Charlie starts from 1 (Keyes probably never even gave it a thought), but I wanted to find a meaning in this too, my own. The way I see it, Charlie is starting a journey: riport 1 is a starting point, but of one particular moment of his life. Before it there’s a riport 0, made of everything he lived up to then. riport 1 is the entrance to a new maze, because that’s exactly how I see life: a series of mazes, some more complex than others, where we’re constantly looking for a light, a meaning, answers, but often end up running into obstacles, wrong choices, and sometimes getting lost, just like Charlie does.

But without dwelling too much on Charlie and on what this book left me, I want to tell the world and myself that this is the starting point of a journey.

I’m surrounded by thoughts, ideas, questions, passions, emotions, hobbies. Work, family, computer science, sport, health, culture…each of these is a potential goal, a destination that, taken on its own, I could maybe even reach easily. And yet I feel stuck in a sort of bubble: every time I try to get closer to one, I drift away from the others, then I get distracted, change direction and in the end I finish nothing. It’s exactly this wandering of mine, this jumping from one thing to another, that keeps me away from all of them.

riport 1 wants to be the opposite: a certainty, something finally finished. And progris riport wants to be my space, the place where I prove to myself (and to whoever wants to read) that I’ve done something, whether it’s about university, computer science, photography, spirituality, sport…any passion or experience, anything that crosses my mind.

So, knowing that what’s ahead of me is a path full of obstacles and mistakes, but also of successes and, sooner or later, a few moments of being lost, I lay the foundations for what’s coming.
